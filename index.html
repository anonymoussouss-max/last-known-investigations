Case Files — 001

What I take on

Three kinds of cases make up most of the work. If yours doesn't fit neatly into one, tell me about it anyway — most real cases don't.

01 / MISSING PERSONS

Locating someone who's gone quiet

A family member, a runaway, someone who's cut off contact on their own terms or someone else's. I work last known addresses, financial trails, and digital footprints to find where the trail actually leads.

02 / BACKGROUND CHECKS

Knowing who you're dealing with

Pre-employment, tenant screening, new relationships, or a business partner you've only just met. Public records, litigation history, and verified employment — run clean, documented, and admissible.

03 / SKIP TRACE & LOCATE

Finding someone who moved on purpose

A debtor, a witness, an heir who can't be reached. Database access and field verification most people don't have, used to close the distance fast.
